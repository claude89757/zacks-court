# Zacks Court 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 3 日 · Effective: November 3, 2026

## 中文

Zacks Court 不收集、不上传、不出售任何个人信息。

- **没有账号，没有云服务**。App 不连接开发者的服务器，也不包含广告、统计或第三方追踪 SDK。
- **录像与分析结果只保存在你的设备上**。机位上的录像、逐拍结果、标记和诊断记录存放在 App 的本地目录里；在 iPhone 上可以通过「文件」App 访问。删除 App 会删除这些数据。你也可以在 App 里删除单条记录或清理录像。
- **设备之间的连接只在你的局域网内**。两台设备在同一 Wi-Fi、个人热点或点对点 Wi-Fi 上直接连接。App 看板之间的连接经 TLS 加密；浏览器看板走局域网明文 HTTP，同一网络里能抓包的人可以看到逐拍数据和预览画面。数据不会经过开发者或任何第三方。
- **相机**：用于机位录像和分析画面，以及扫描配对二维码。画面在设备本机处理。
- **本地网络**：用于连接你的另一台设备，或让同一网络里的浏览器打开看板。
- **照片（仅添加）**：只在你点「存到相册」时把视频片段添加到相册；App 不读取你的相册。
- **分享**：你主动分享的视频片段或记录文件，由你选择的接收方处理，适用其隐私政策。
- **儿童**：App 不收集任何人的信息，包括儿童。

本政策如有变化，会在这里更新生效日期。联系方式：见 App Store 页面上的开发者支持链接。

## English

Zacks Court does not collect, upload or sell any personal information.

- **No account, no cloud.** The app never contacts the developer's servers and contains no advertising, analytics or third-party tracking SDKs.
- **Recordings and results stay on your devices.** Recordings, shot data, markers and diagnostic logs are stored in the app's local folder; on iPhone you can reach it through the Files app. Deleting the app deletes this data. You can also delete single sessions or clear recordings in the app.
- **Devices connect only on your local network.** Two devices connect directly over the same Wi-Fi, a Personal Hotspot or peer-to-peer Wi-Fi. Links between app displays are TLS-encrypted; the browser display uses plain HTTP on the local network, so someone capturing traffic on that network can see shot data and the preview. No data passes through the developer or any third party.
- **Camera:** used to record and analyse the court, and to scan pairing QR codes. Frames are processed on the device.
- **Local network:** used to connect to your other device, or to let a browser on the same network open the display.
- **Photos (add-only):** used only when you tap "Save to Photos" to add a video clip; the app never reads your library.
- **Sharing:** clips or files you choose to share are handled by the recipient you pick, under their own privacy policy.
- **Children:** the app collects no information from anyone, including children.

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated. Contact: see the developer support link on the App Store page.
